# Frontal attacking creature

Generated with built-in imagegen on a transparent background. The 1448x1086
source is `monster-sheet.png`: four columns, three rows, twelve 362px cells.
`tools/prepare_shooting_monster.py` exports the individual runtime PNGs.

Prompt: Production horror videogame character animation sprite atlas. Exactly
four columns by three rows, twelve isolated full-body cells of the same
photoreal inhuman winter creature. Lean tall gray leathery skin, long arms,
hunched shoulders, amber eyes, ragged dark charcoal coat and trousers, bare
clawlike hands and feet. Fictional and non-graphic, no blood, gore, wounds,
victim or weapons. Direct front view, fixed camera, cold blue key light and
restrained red side rim for a snowy city at night. Entire body and both feet
visible, identical character size and identity, constant ground baseline.
Frames 1-8: seamless running-toward-camera gait with alternating legs and arms.
Frames 9-12: fists raised, fists moving forward, both hands contacting an
invisible barrier, then arms recovering. No barrier, zoom, labels, text,
gridlines, scenery, ground plane or cast-shadow plate. Actual transparency.
