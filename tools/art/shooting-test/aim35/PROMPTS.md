# 35-direction aiming sprite sources

Generated with built-in imagegen. Five pitch atlases, seven yaw columns, idle/fire rows. Export with `python tools/prepare_aim35.py`. Runtime assets: `game/images/shooting_test/aim35/`.

## up-high

Use case: stylized-concept. Production first-person game sprite atlas using the reference ONLY for character hands, dark pistol, black sleeves, realistic material style and red rim lighting. Make an EXACT equal-cell 7 column by 2 row atlas, 14 separate poses. Cells are tall rectangles, with the whole atlas about 2.6:1. EVERY cell has identical fixed camera, same TWO hands holding the same gun, and forearms extending completely to the bottom edge of its OWN cell. Large clear padding at the top of each cell, do not let any pixels cross into neighboring cells. All poses in this atlas have pitch 18 degrees UPWARD, the muzzle visibly points toward the upper part of the screen, wrists angled upward in 3D. Across EACH row the yaw directions are -30,-20,-10,0,+10,+20,+30 degrees, leftmost aiming left and rightmost aiming right. TOP ROW: idle. BOTTOM ROW: identical direction and framing as the top row with a small muzzle flash. Pistol and wrists turn in actual perspective, show different side surfaces, NEVER roll the flat picture sideways. Each pose maintains a steady common wrist base at the cell's lower center. Hands do not move across the cell. Preserve full sleeves down to the bottom, no chopped side edges, enough side margin. Photorealistic clean game render. Transparent alpha background. No text, no labels, no grid, no environment, no extra objects, no targets, no blood. Important: this is the UP-HIGH pitch set; each barrel must visibly aim UP relative to the hands, not level.
